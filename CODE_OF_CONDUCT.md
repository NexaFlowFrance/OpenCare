# Code of conduct

## In short

OpenCare exists because people take care of someone. Bring the same patience here.

## What is expected

- Assume the person on the other side is tired, and busy, and doing their best.
- Be specific and factual in reviews. Comment on the code, not on the person.
- Accept that a maintainer can say no to a feature and still be grateful for the idea.
- Respect privacy. Never paste real health data, real names, addresses or photos of a
  cared-for person into an issue, a pull request or a screenshot. Anonymise first.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual attention, sustained
disruption, publishing someone's private information, and any behaviour that would make a
reasonable person leave the project.

## Reporting

Write to contact@nexaflow.fr. Reports are read by the maintainers only and are not shared
further without the reporter's agreement. A report is acted on even if the behaviour
happened somewhere else and involves this community.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close pull requests, and block accounts. They will
explain why when they do, unless explaining would expose the reporter.

## Scope

This applies to every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and any place where someone represents OpenCare.

Adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, kept short on purpose.
